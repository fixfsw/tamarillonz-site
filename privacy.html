Privacy

Tamarillo Timer respects your privacy. All of your settings stay on your device — they're saved locally and never sent anywhere. There's no account and no login. The app doesn't track you or "phone home": it makes no network connections of its own, bundles no analytics or advertising code, and shows no ads. This website uses the privacy friendly analytics service GoatCounter. It does not collect any personal information.
